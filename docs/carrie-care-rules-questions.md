## Capacity and home

1. What is the **maximum number of dogs** you take at once (daycare vs overnight if different)?
2. Are dogs **ever left alone** in the home? If yes, for how long, and under what conditions (crate, free roam, cameras)?
3. Who else lives in or regularly visits the home (people, other pets)? Should that be stated on the site?
4. Is the backyard **fully fenced** with no escape gaps? Any areas dogs are not allowed?

## Vaccines, health, and packing

5. Which **vaccines or titres** are required before a stay?
6. Any other health rules (flea/tick, heartworm, recent illness, intact dogs)?
7. What should owners **bring** (food, meds, bed, leash, tags)? What do you provide?
8. Will you give **medications** (oral, injectables, special diets)? Any limits?

## Daily rhythm and updates

9. Rough **day schedule** (meals, potty, play, rest) you are comfortable publishing?
10. How often do you send **updates or photos** during a stay (e.g. midday photo)? Is that a promise or “when you can”?
11. Typical **drop-off / pickup** windows?

## If something goes wrong

12. How do you **reach the owner** first (Rover messages, call, text)? Preferred order?
13. Which **vet or emergency clinic** do you use if the owner cannot be reached? May we name it on the site?
14. Is there a **backup person** who can cover if you cannot (illness, emergency)? May we mention that there is one without naming them?
15. What happens if a dog becomes **sick or injured** during a stay (who authorizes treatment/cost)?

## Who a stay is for (and who it is not)

16. Which dogs are a **great fit** (first-timers, seniors, puppies, multi-dog households, high-energy, anxious)?
17. Which situations do you **decline** after a meet (e.g. dog-dog aggression, cannot be around other dogs, severe separation anxiety you cannot support)?
18. Do you take **puppies**? From what age? Intact dogs?
19. Any breed or size limits worth stating?

## Trust signals (only if true)

20. Years caring for dogs / sitting (number you are happy to show)?
21. Approximate **repeat clients** or total stays (only if you have a real figure)?
22. Rover **background check** — yes/no, and OK to say so on the site?
23. **Pet first aid / CPR** certification — yes/no, year if useful?
24. Liability **insurance** beyond Rover — yes/no, and OK to mention without policy details?

## FAQ leftovers

25. How does the **meet-and-greet** work in practice (length, at your home, what you look for)?
26. **Cancellation / change** policy (or “follow Rover’s policy”)?
27. Food guarding, resource guarding, or crate preferences — what do you do with that information once the owner discloses it?
