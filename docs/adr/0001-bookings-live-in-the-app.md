# Bookings live in the app

A stay is a Booking in Care Bear Daycare. The public site starts that Booking (register or sign in, then `/account/book`). The Rover listing is one discovery link for finding Carrie and reading reviews there. It is not a second booking desk.

We considered leaving the public Rover buttons in place, and keeping both desks live for old versus new clients. Those split the record of a stay, so the app would never be the place Carrie trusts on the day.
