# Care Bear Daycare

The record of Carrie's in-home dog care: who the dogs belong to, and the stays booked with her.

## Language

**Booking**:
A stay of one service for one pet parent's dogs over a date window, recorded in this daycare.
_Avoid_: Rover booking, reservation

**Pet parent**:
The person who owns the dogs and holds the account for them.
_Avoid_: owner, client, customer

**Rover listing**:
Carrie's public Rover profile, used so people can find her and read reviews there. A stay is not booked on it.
_Avoid_: booking link, Rover checkout

**Standing care**:
A dog's feeding, medications, temperament, vet, and notes. It stays true from one booking to the next.
_Avoid_: care update, parent note

**Care update**:
What happened during one booking: potty, food, play, whether meds were given, and a short note. A later update does not change an earlier one.
_Avoid_: standing care, parent note

**Care-update email**:
A pet parent's choice to be emailed when a care update is added on any of their bookings. Off until they opt in.
_Avoid_: notification, alert, subscribe

**Staff name**:
The name shown for the staff member who wrote a care update: given name, a space, then the first letter of the family name and a period. Chris Cross is Chris C.
_Avoid_: username, email, user id

**Parent note**:
A note the pet parent attaches to one booking, such as a pickup time or a gate code.
_Avoid_: standing care, care update

**Not social**:
A dog that cannot share the home with another family's dogs. One not-social dog makes the booking exclusive on every date it occupies.
_Avoid_: unfriendly, aggressive, bad with dogs

**Exclusive stay**:
A booking that occupies its dates alone because at least one of its dogs is not social.
_Avoid_: private booking, solo stay

**Vaccine record**:
A named vaccine on a dog, with an optional expiry date. It does not say which vaccines are required, and an expired record does not block a booking.
_Avoid_: vaccine requirement, clearance
